What Our Ant Control Service Includes

Every visit has four parts, from the first look to the follow up.

Initial Inspection

We check inside and out for trails, nests, sawdust, and wet wood. We identify the ant species, because the treatment depends on it.

Targeted Treatment

We treat the nest and the trails by the product label. Baits work for small sugar loving ants. Nest treatments work for carpenter ants.

Prevention

We point out leaks, damp wood, and gaps. We also tell you what to move or trim. Carpenter ants often reach the roof from tree limbs, and Illinois Extension advises keeping firewood off the ground and away from the house.

Follow Up Visit

We recheck after 2 weeks to confirm the ants are gone. If they return, we return.

Methods and Standards

Integrated Pest Management

The EPA describes this as prevention first, with pesticides used only as needed.

Find the Nest

University of Kentucky Extension says the best way to control carpenter ants is to find and destroy the nests, and not to spray ants you see.

Moisture Control and Sealing

Illinois Extension says carpenter ants are drawn to damp areas and often nest in wood tied to roof leaks and leaky pipes. Fixing the wet spot removes the draw.

How Ant Control Works in Coeur d'Alene

Frequently Asked Questions

Straight answers from our technicians.

How do you get rid of ants in Coeur d'Alene?
Find the nest and treat the colony. For carpenter ants, fix the wet wood that drew them. For small sugar ants, use baits that workers carry home. Sprays that only kill the ants you see often leave the nest alone.
Do ants cause health problems?
Most ants here are a nuisance. They can contaminate food, and carpenter ants point to damp wood. See a doctor if someone has a reaction to a bite or sting.
How much does ant control cost in Coeur d'Alene?
Most visits cost between $180 and $450. Price depends on the ant type, home size, and how many nests we find. We give the price in writing before work starts.
When should you call an ant control professional?
Call if ants return after a week of cleaning and baits, if you see sawdust or winged ants indoors, or if large black ants appear at night.
Carpenter ants or termites?
Carpenter ants have narrow waists and bent antennae, and leave coarse sawdust. Termites do not. We check for both.
